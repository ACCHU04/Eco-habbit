Why EcoHabit?

Everything you need
to go circular

One platform. Multiple ways to reduce waste and earn rewards.

🛍️

Marketplace

Buy and sell second-hand items within your campus community. From textbooks to lab equipment — find what you need affordably.

🎁

Donate & Gift

Pass on items you no longer need. Donating earns bonus eco-points and helps juniors get a head start without spending money.

♻️

Recycle Hub

Locate nearest recycling drop-off points. Our AI categorises your waste and tells you exactly how to dispose of it responsibly.

🤖

AI Matching

MobileNetV2-powered image recognition identifies items instantly. Smart matching suggests the best buyers or donors for your listing.

🏆

Gamified Learning

Earn eco-points for every sustainable action. Climb leaderboards, unlock badges, and learn entrepreneurship through real transactions.

🌍

Impact Tracker

See your real-world environmental impact — CO₂ saved, plastic diverted, money saved. Your actions visualised beautifully.
